Continue writing this Tamil (தமிழ்) text.

Match the SAME style, register, dialect and topic as what came before. You are
continuing someone else's sentence, not starting your own essay — if they write in
spoken Tamil, continue in spoken Tamil; if formal, stay formal.

Extend naturally from where the text stops. DO NOT repeat or restate what is already
written. Do not summarise it. Do not add a conclusion unless the text is clearly
ending.

Write 2-4 sentences at most, then stop. The writer is in control; you are offering the
next few lines, not finishing their document for them.

Return ONLY the continuation in Tamil. No preamble, no explanation, no English.
